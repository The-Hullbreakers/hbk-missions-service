# Feature Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Domain Foundation

- Category/type/subtype taxonomy.
- Versioned definitions and offers.
- Curated pools, publication modes and rotation policy.
- Active limit policy.
- Exclusion by active template.
- Single/team and lifecycle contracts.
- Objectives, confirmations and ledger.
- Seasons and standings.

## Phase 2 — Mission Board MVP

- Administration and publication of the pool.
- Scheduled rotation of predefined offers.
- Discovery, filters and eligibility.
- Consistent acceptance and active/history views.
- Manual review of evidence.
- Bounty Hunting as the first vertical category.

## Phase 3 — Competitive Season

- Merits per unit and bonuses.
- Individual/team leaderboards.
- Disputes, adjustments and freezes.
- Completion and placement awards towards Achievements.

## Phase 4 — More Careers

Mining, Salvage, Hauling, Racing, Medical Rescue, Exploration and Security, each with specific objectives and policies approved by SDD.

## Future Candidates

- Mission chains and prerequisites.
- Daily/weekly contracts and advanced cooldowns.
- Reputation with mission givers.
- Community goals and special events.
- External multimedia evidence.
- Automatic confirmation sources.
- Divisions and leagues.
- Statistics and advanced antifarming.
- Material rewards coordinated with Inventory.
- Optional references to the SC Tools catalog.

Each candidate requires a delta against the proprietary canonical capability; not included upfront in v1 implementation.
